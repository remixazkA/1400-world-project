# Results and examples

This gallery documents representative outputs from Project 1400. Its purpose is not only to display visually successful regions, but also to show how results are evaluated, which defects appear during procedural generation and how later iterations respond to them.

Each example will identify the geographic area, the stage of the pipeline, the relevant inputs and the quality criteria used during review. Where comparable outputs exist, before-and-after images will be presented together.

## Regional examples

The regional gallery will be expanded with geographically diverse cases rather than concentrating only on the easiest or most visually attractive areas. Planned examples include:

- densely settled temperate regions;
- mountain systems and narrow valleys;
- arid and sparsely populated areas;
- river basins and deltas;
- islands and fragmented coastlines;
- steppe, tundra and tropical environments.

## Validation cases

Selected case studies will document both successful results and instructive failures. Examples may include:

- elongated territories and narrow slivers;
- artificial straight borders;
- incorrect river or mountain crossing behaviour;
- fragmented or disconnected territories;
- abrupt changes in density between neighbouring regions;
- improvements produced by revised parameters or cleanup rules.

## Selection criteria

Images included here should contribute evidence about the system. They are selected according to four principles:

1. **Representativeness:** the example reveals behaviour that matters beyond one isolated location.
2. **Traceability:** the region and processing stage can be identified.
3. **Comparability:** where possible, the image can be compared with another iteration or expected behaviour.
4. **Honesty:** defects and limitations are documented alongside visually strong outputs.

The gallery will grow as additional regional results are reviewed and prepared for publication.
